# Vision Brief

## Who is the customer?
Insurance customers seeking a fast, simple, and user-friendly way to estimate insurance coverage costs.

## What pain does Evergreen Quote remove?
Evergreen Quote reduces the time and effort required to obtain insurance premium estimates by providing immediate quote calculations through a modern web application.

## What does success look like this week?
Deliver a working Evergreen Quote React application that successfully loads, calculates quotes, passes type checking, completes a production build, and is merged into the main branch through a reviewed pull request.

## What are we not doing this week?
We are not building new functionality from scratch, redesigning the application, or making architectural changes. Our focus is assembling, configuring, validating, and delivering the provided solution components.
